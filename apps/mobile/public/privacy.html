Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · עברית

This policy explains what FinPace ("the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you control it. It covers the app and the site finpace-app.vercel.app, and is written to meet the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 (including Amendment 13) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible for your data

The app is run by Tomer Chibotero, a private individual in Ramat Gan, Israel, who is the database controller under Israeli law and the controller under the GDPR.

For any privacy question, including exercising your rights: tomerchibo01@gmail.com.

The app never receives card numbers, bank credentials or account balances. It has no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.

You are under no legal obligation to provide any data. Without an email address you can't sign in, and without expenses the app has nothing to show; everything else is optional.

AI suggestions

AI suggestions are off by default and run only with the household's consent (legal basis: consent). When on, the app suggests a category for an expense and writes the monthly report. Suggestions decide nothing about you: each can be changed, and no automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made (GDPR Article 22). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → AI suggestions.

Who can see your data

Everyone in your household sees all of the household's expenses, budgets and reports; that is the point of a shared household. No one outside the household can see it. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Service providers

The app relies on these providers. Each receives only what it needs to provide its service:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, server functions). Data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Vercel (hosting the site and app). Receives the technical logs of each visit, such as IP address. Its servers are worldwide, including the United States.
  • Google (Gmail), to send sign-in codes by email. Receives your email address and the code.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI), only if your household turned on AI suggestions. Sent: merchant names, expense titles, amounts, currencies, the card name Apple Wallet shows (e.g. "Max Visa") and category names. Card numbers, notes, member names and email addresses are never sent. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its API.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla push services, to deliver budget alerts, if you turned them on.
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank, for exchange rates. No personal data is sent.

Transfers outside Israel and the EU

Your main data is stored in the EU. Some providers (Vercel, Google, Anthropic) are in the United States, so data may be transferred there. Transfers are made under each provider's data processing terms, which include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified. Israel has an EU adequacy decision, so data may move between the EU and Israel.

What is stored on your device

The app uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps in your browser or phone only what it needs to work: your sign-in, the language and appearance you chose, a draft of an expense you started without a connection, and the app's files so it opens offline. All of these are strictly necessary for the service, and are removed when you sign out or clear the site's data in your browser.

How long we keep data

  • For as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted expenses are kept hidden so a deletion can be undone, and are erased with the household.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from the database at once. Copies in the provider's automatic backups are erased when those backups expire.
  • Providers keep technical logs for a short period, under their own retention policies.

Your rights

Under Israeli law and the GDPR you have these rights. Most you can exercise yourself in the app:

  • Access: to know what data is held about you and receive a copy.
  • Portability: Settings → Export expenses downloads all expenses as a CSV file.
  • Correction: edit any expense, category or budget in the app.
  • Erasure: Settings → Delete account. Your account is deleted at once. A household where you're the only member is deleted with all its data; a shared household stays with the other members, and your name is removed from it.
  • Withdrawing consent: for AI suggestions and notifications, at any time, in Settings. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before it.
  • Restriction and objection to processing based on legitimate interest.

For any other request: tomerchibo01@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or, if you live in the EU, to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

Permissions are enforced by the database on every request, so one household can never read another's data. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Shortcut tokens and invite codes are stored only as hashes. If a breach that may harm you is discovered, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. For a material change, such as a new use of your data, we will tell you in advance in the app or by email.
