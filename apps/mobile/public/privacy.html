What the app collects

DataWhy
Email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code.
Display nameSo members of your household see who added what.
Expenses: merchant name as charged, amount, currency, date, card label you type, notes, categoryThe core of the app: tracking, budgets and reports.

The app never receives card numbers, bank credentials or account balances. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who can see your data

Everyone in your household sees all of the household's expenses, budgets and reports. That is the purpose of a shared household. Nobody outside your household can see it.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, server functions). Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Anthropic (Claude AI). Only if your household turns on AI suggestions: merchant names, amounts and category names are sent to suggest categories and write the monthly report. No names, emails or card data are sent. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not train its models on API data by default.
  • Email delivery ([email provider]) to send sign-in codes.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla push services to deliver budget alerts, if you turned them on.
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank for exchange rates. No personal data is sent.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Deleted expenses are kept hidden for undo and removed with the household.

Your rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Export Expenses downloads everything as CSV.
  • Correction: edit any expense, category or budget in the app.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete Account. Your account is deleted immediately. A household you are alone in is deleted with all its data; a household you share stays with the other members, and your name is removed from it.
  • Withdraw AI consent: Settings → AI suggestions, at any time.

You can also contact us at [privacy contact email], or complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (or your local EU supervisory authority).

Security

Access is enforced in the database for every request, so one household can never read another's data. Shortcut tokens and invite codes are stored only as hashes.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
